CHANGELOG

What changed in the Neyrith Player's Guide, newest first

Back to the guide

Each entry lists rule and content changes that affect characters. Small wording fixes aren't listed. If you built a character before a date below, check that entry.

2026-11-18: campaign traits and languages

  • Campaign traits: a first list of ten, each a reason to go into Neyrith. Take one as one of your two traits. See Campaign traits.
  • Languages: Neyrithi is Neyrith's language, and almost everything written inside the walls uses it. Celestial, Infernal and Abyssal don't exist here. See Languages.
  • Session 0: each character's arrival in Gatewater gets a short scene before the first session.

2026-11-17 (later): loyalties replace alignment

  • No alignment. Characters choose three ranked loyalties, using Pathfinder Unchained's Removing Alignment rules. Good and evil effects become radiant and shadow, smite works by loyalty, and options that pick disciplines by alignment use your faith's aspects. See Loyalties.
  • The Hearthfather: aspects (Order and Mercy), tenets and banned spells added. Nobility replaces his Law domain.
  • Bastion: aspects, tenets, forbidden powers, and what happens when a bastion breaks the tenets (see Third-Party Changes).

2026-11-17: what's allowed is now broad

  • Content is now broadly allowed. Psionic, Path of War and akashic content from any publisher is open, not just the options this guide named. Tell the GM what third-party options you're using; the GM may adjust them. See the new What's Allowed section, with its short "Ruled out" and "Ask the GM first" lists.
  • Third-Party Options is now Third-Party Changes: it lists only reviewed options and their changes.
  • Added: changes for Psikasha Expanded — Intrusive Thoughts (Bastion, Savitur, Psychovoric Retribution) and notes for Titles of Providence.
  • Veils: other publishers' veils are open; flag them for the GM.
  • Humans: Neyrith's people were dahlians, a psionic human bloodline; the dahlian alternate racial traits are available. Ordinary humans come from beyond the region.
  • Epilektoi archetypes: the Thornbearer and the Populist (Intoxicating Petal), with changes under Third-Party Changes.

2026-11-16 (night): more classes, disciplines and a new race

  • New classes: Genesis, Skipper and Warpmind (Spoonbender Games' Psionics+); Eclipse, Nexus and Radiant Retold; and the Mysteriarch, Astradhari and Hearthkeeper (Azoth Games). Each has changes listed under Third-Party Options.
  • New race: the kambian.
  • New disciplines: Sagitta Stellaris and Intoxicating Petal, with changes.
  • New prestige classes: Phrenic Slayer, Thrallherd, Metamorph, Telekinetic Weaponmaster, Dark Tempest and Psychic Fist (Dreamscarred Press, missed in the first pass), plus Astral Fist, Collider, Phantom, Soulbuster and Warpknight (Psionics+). The meditant psychic warrior counts as a monk for the two fist classes. See Prestige classes.
  • Veil rule: Retold versions where they exist; Dreamscarred veils without one are fine; other publishers' veils on an allowed class's list need GM approval.

2026-11-16 (evening): prestige classes

  • Prestige classes are settled. Dreamscarred Press prestige classes are in, plus Black Templar (Retold), Bloodfuser (Retold) and the Veilbreaker from Studio M—, with a few changes. See Prestige classes. Cerebremancer, Bladecaster and Mage Hunter are out (arcane spells). Entry requirements tied to the world (a teacher, book, item or event) are enforced in play.
  • Removed feats as prerequisites: a new Elephant in the Room note in House Rules says how to meet requirements that name a feat Elephant in the Room removed or merged.
  • Mind blade: its own weapon group for Weapon Focus and the other weapon feats (GM Rulings).
  • The Plane of Shadow now exists, as the dark flipside of the Astral (The Planes).
  • The Snake Charmer and Resonant Song archetypes aren't part of the setting, but you can ask the GM about one.

2026-11-16 (later): third-party options

  • Akashic classes are now the Akasha Retold versions (Studio M—): Daevic, Guru, Vizier and Rajah Retold replace the Dreamscarred originals, and Retold veils replace the original veils where a Retold version exists. Affects existing akashic characters, especially rajahs: a Retold rajah gets maneuvers only through the Batal path, and the original Crossroads choices (including Crown of the Sky Sovereign) are gone. Rajah level no longer counts as base attack bonus for feat prerequisites in general: Rajah Retold treats rajah levels as full BAB only for attacks made through vassals, and for feats used with those attacks. Re-check feat plans that relied on it (for example Snap Shot).
  • New: Third-Party Options lists everything allowed beyond Paizo and Dreamscarred Press, with changes: rajah paths and Batal rulings, the Xeshim and Dancer of the Seven Veils archetypes, the epilektoi, Quantum, Malleo, a set of rajah titles, Dahlia's Psionic Halo, Bonds of Freedom, Moon and Tides (Surging Shark, The Wave, Mermaid's Tail), and Studio M—'s 13 anti-essence psionic powers.
  • New: GM Rulings: the Psychic Armory soulknife (panoply-off tactics, and a note that Wisdom comes only from Focused Offense).
  • Malefex added to the class list (it's a Dreamscarred Press class and was always allowed).
  • Mythic: still not used; it may be added later.
  • New pages: this changelog, and a page for AI assistants.

2026-11-16: full house rules and feats

  • Feats: Elephant in the Room v3.0 in full, replacing the Core Rulebook feat chapter. Weapon Proficiency and weapon feats work by fighter weapon group. The full text is on the new Feats page.
  • House rules section now holds the complete list (hit points, fractional bonuses, background skills, hero points or Anti-Hero, feats). There's nothing more to ask the GM for.
  • Removed: the "alignment steps" note. It's how alignment works as written, not a house rule.
  • Starting wealth: 1,000 gp (a 2nd-level character's wealth), spending no more than half on any one item.

2026-11-15: first draft

  • First version of the guide: the setting, races, classes, character creation and house rules. Class and race names link to their rules.
